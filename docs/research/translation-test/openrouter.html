UK gov to keep common charger rules aligned with EU

The UK government will not introduce new common charger regulations for Great Britain at this time. It will keep CE marking to support trade.

The UK government has published its response to a call for evidence on a common charger for mobile phones and other portable devices. The Office for Product Safety and Standards received 104 substantive responses from consumer groups, manufacturers, charities, retailers and the public. Overall, 86% of respondents said any regulations should align with the EU and Northern Ireland to avoid costs. However, 35% considered regulation unnecessary because USB-C is already the industry default. The government does not intend to introduce common charger regulations in Great Britain at this time. It will continue to recognise CE marking to support trade and market alignment. The government will keep technological and regulatory developments under review and reconsider its position if necessary.

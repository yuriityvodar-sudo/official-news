# Sources review (#115, #101)

Checked 21 Oct 2026 from Cloudflare's network (sites treat a datacenter address differently from a laptop), with the fetcher's User-Agent `OfficialNewsBot/0.1 (+https://github.com/yuriityvodar-sudo/official-news)`, through a temporary Worker run with `wrangler dev --remote`. Every new or changed `config` was then run through the fetcher itself (`runFetcher`, Vitest) on the answers saved from Cloudflare that day. Labels: **checked** (from Cloudflare), **ran** (the fetcher on the saved answers), **measured** (the live database).

## Why

**Measured**, 14 days to 21 Oct: 260 news articles in the feed, 82% from 7 countries (BR 64, AR 33, MX 31, GB 27, PL 22, KR 20, ZA 17); 5 countries had nothing. The median text the AI got was short where the list gives only a title: South Africa 93 characters (PDF only), Mexico 241 (titles), Poland's Journal of Laws 259 (titles), Brazil 333 (bill summaries), Argentina 365 (abstracts). Germany and the EU already get the full text since 19 and 20 Oct (new items 3,000 to 10,000 characters). Ukraine saved nothing since 16 Oct: `kmu.gov.ua` shows the fetcher a Radware CAPTCHA.

The aim (Roman, 21 Oct): official decisions from more countries, with their text, and fewer routine entries, filtered at the source rather than by the AI.

## Expected effect

**Estimate**, per week: US +5 to 10, UA +2, ZA +1 a month, BR from about 42 (mostly bills) to about 8 to 12 decisions (5 decrees, 1 to 2 provisional measures, laws in session), MX fewer routine entries and full texts, PL's Journal of Laws from about 14 to about 9, without ministers' regulations. The AI still hides what is not news.

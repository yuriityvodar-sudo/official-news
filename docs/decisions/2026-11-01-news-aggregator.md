# The feed as an aggregator: every country's own outlets, headlines and links only (#230)

- **Decision:** The feed works as news aggregators' regional editions do, for a reader from any country: that country's own news outlets, their headlines grouped into stories and ranked for that country, with our official decisions and events among them (the plan in `docs/plan.md`, The aggregator, four steps). This pull request is step 1: 107 outlets in 21 countries, the world and the EU (`docs/outlets.csv`), each kept as its headline, its link and its time, never its text, picture or a summary of it. They live in their own table, `outlets`, one row per outlet with its last 48 hours of headlines as JSON (`docs/contract.md`, Database): the API's country filters read every row of `sources`, and one row per headline would cost about 50,000 rows written a day, and reading them per country for the ranking could pass D1's 5 million rows read a day. The fetcher checks each outlet's feed every 15 minutes, as it checks a source (robots.txt every hour, a changed feed parsed, an unchanged one costing one request), after the sources, and calls at most 30 a run: Cloudflare stops a request at 32 Worker invocations, and with outlets every 10 minutes 24 minutes a day would have gone past it (a simulation over a day of the sources' and outlets' turns: at most 28 calls a minute at 15). An outlet's feed is read for its headlines only, never an item's text or picture: some outlets put whole articles in their feed, and the biggest (elDiario.es, 1.4 MB) took 24 ms of CPU to read with its texts and 4 ms without them (on a laptop; the median outlet 0.4 ms, against the 10 ms a call has). `GET /headlines?country=XX` gives a country's newest headlines with their outlets, and `/health` counts the outlets per status.
- **Why:** Roman, 1 Nov, after a For you for Czechia with no Czech news in it (China's State Council, a Wikipedia event in Germany and the US Federal Register on top): «как источник Радио Свобода пользователю из Чехии или венгрии будет интересен????? в чем проблема? почему ты не можешь составить ленту как агрегатор???» and «чехию или венгрию я назвал просто условным примером / поэтому и должно быть правильное ранжирование / я уже об этом писал / для пользователя из разной страны свой источник и свое ранжирование / так как это делают агрегаторы». The fixed principle (#230, 29 Oct) already allows other media as a headline and a link, never their text, which EU law (Directive 2019/790, art. 15) exempts. No letters and no paid licences («пользуем то что есть бесплатно без проблем или сложностей»): an outlet whose feed or robots.txt is closed to us is left out, and one that asks us to stop is taken out.

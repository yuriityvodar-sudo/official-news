# A country's stories built within a call's CPU, and a failed build waits (#230)

- **Decision:** Building a country's stories takes less CPU. Each headline's words are read once and kept while the call's isolate lives (a country is built every few minutes from mostly the same headlines). Words are numbers within a call, a story's length is kept as it grows instead of summed again, and times and links are ordered by their code units instead of `localeCompare`. A build groups at most the newest 300 headlines (was 450) and keeps the 50 weightiest stories (was 100). The stories come out the same: on the 4,124 headlines of 1 Nov every country's stories are the same sets of headlines as before, and only which of two equal headlines is the title can differ. A country whose build failed (its call stopped by a limit) waits 30 minutes before it is tried again (`country_stories.failed_at`, migration `0030`), keeping the stories it has.
- **Why:** On Workers the builds of 1 Nov took 38 ms of CPU for India's 450 headlines and 15 to 30 ms for most countries, against the free plan's 10 ms a call, which Cloudflare enforces once a Worker goes over it consistently (Yurii's check of #281). On a laptop a whole build now takes 2.9 ms for India (was 7.9), 2.2 to 2.4 ms for Germany, Spain and Ukraine (were 4.3 to 5.3). Yurii's review of #281: a build that keeps failing would otherwise take a slot every run and never get a row.
- **Status:** Proposed (Roman's Claude, after Yurii's check of #281)

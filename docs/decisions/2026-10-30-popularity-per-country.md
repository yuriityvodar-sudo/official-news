# Popularity per country (#230 step 4)

- **Decision:** How much each country's readers look an item up on Wikipedia and how much its own media publish about it, 0 to 2, for For you's score (step 5). The summarizer's one call also names up to 2 English Wikipedia articles and up to 4 English keywords (`wikipedia`, `keywords`); every 4th minute the summarizer's run checks one released item of the last 72 hours (again after 12 hours): its English page's language links give its titles in other languages, Wikimedia's pageviews API their views a day (the most since the item against the median of the week before: up to 1.5), and one GDELT search the outlets of each country in 48 hours (up to 1, with up to 3 links: headline, outlet and link only). Stored in `article_views` and `article_media` (migration `0024`). A country's language stands for its readers (cs for Czechia, English for the US and the UK alike)
- **Why:** Roman, 30 Oct: «строй полностью доконца что запланировали… если что потом поправим». Measured on 30 Oct: Lagarde's resignation took her Czech page from a usual 6 views a day to 545. Wikipedia's API is closed to robots (robots.txt), its pages are not; the pageviews API is open data. GDELT answered every request on 30 Oct, from a laptop and from Cloudflare, with 429 or with no articles at all (even "Ukraine" over a day), so the check takes it whenever it answers and counts on Wikipedia meanwhile

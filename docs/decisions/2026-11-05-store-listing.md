# The App Store listing: "Outer News: AI Brief", out as soon as Apple approves it (#137)

- **Decision:** on the App Store the app is "Outer News: AI Brief", subtitle "What the news means for you"; on the home screen it stays "Outer News". It is free, in 174 countries (all but China mainland), for iPhone and iPad (Mac and Vision Pro off). The version is released automatically once Apple approves it, so there is no release date to set: Yurii learns of it from App Store Connect's e-mail at approval. So the phone check of the release-candidate build comes before the submission, and the server must already be live for that build when it is submitted.
- **Why:** Roman's choices of 4 and 5 Nov («автоматически ставлю»; «Mac + Vision Pro» off). Yurii agreed to the name on #320. China mainland needs a licence for news apps. The store texts are general marketing, with no talk of sources (Roman, 4 Nov).
- **Status:** Proposed (Roman's Claude, on Roman's word of 5 Nov)

# The AI chat's limit is a week's usage, shown as usage, never as a count of questions (#146)

- **Decision:** a subscription gives a week's usage, what 20 questions a day cost (`WEEKLY_MICROS`, $0.28 of OpenAI cost a week, so at most about $1.20 a month, as before), which comes back every Monday at 00:00 UTC; unused usage does not carry over. The app never shows a count of questions or answers: from 75% of the week's usage used, "80% of this week's usage used · resets Monday" over the field; while extra usage is spent, "Using extra usage"; with nothing left, the card "You've reached this week's usage limit.", "It resets on Monday at 01:00.", "Get more usage". Questions about long documents still use more ("Questions about long documents use more of your usage."), with no number. The paywall says "Weekly usage included". The packs keep their product ids and add weeks of usage: `…questions50` is "+1 week of usage" ($0.99), `…questions200` "+3 weeks of usage" ($2.99); they never expire and are spent after the week's usage. The terms (`termsSubscription`) and the privacy policy (`privacyChat`) say so in all five languages. The API stays backward compatible for builds already out: `/v1/chat/status` keeps `questionsLeft`, `boughtQuestions`, `units` and `resetsAt` (now the next Monday) and adds `usedPercent`, `extraUsage` and `limitReached`; an answer keeps `X-Chat-Remaining` and `X-Chat-Units` and adds `X-Chat-Used` and `X-Chat-Extra`; `/v1/chat/credits` adds `extraUsage`; the `429` says "No usage left this week". `chat_usage.day` holds the week's Monday, with no migration. The Sandbox cap of #319 becomes a week's: 700 questions (100 a day before). This replaces 27 Oct's "20 questions a day" and "packs of 50 and 200 questions" (`docs/decisions.md`) and the daily Sandbox cap of `2026-11-04-chat-for-review.md`.
- **Why:** Roman, 4 Nov: «пользователь покупает weekly usage»; «везде где есть подсчеты сколько сообщений осталось или сколько ответов мы предоставляем — везде это убери и замени на usage»; «у нас же есть limits». Usage reads as a service, as Claude's limits do; counts read as rationing. A week leaves room for a busy day at the same monthly cost.
- **Status:** Proposed (Roman's Claude, on Roman's word of 4 Nov). Russian keeps the English word "usage" (Roman); Ukrainian does the same, Spanish and German say "uso" and "Nutzung", until Roman says otherwise. The 700 a week for the Sandbox cap is proposed, not approved.

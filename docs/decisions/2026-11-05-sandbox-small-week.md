# Sandbox purchases without a team key get a small week, so App Review reaches the packs (#146, #137)

- **Decision:** a Sandbox subscription without a team key, App Review's from the `store` build among them, gets a week's usage of about 3 questions (`SANDBOX_WEEK_MICROS` in `server/src/chat/usage.ts`) instead of the App Store's. After a few questions App Review sees "You've reached this week's usage limit." and "Get more usage", the two packs on the top-up sheet. A pack bought in the Sandbox adds the App Store's weeks, so App Review can go on asking after buying one. The team's keys (`CHAT_SANDBOX_KEY`, `CHAT_SANDBOX_KEY_TESTFLIGHT`) keep the App Store's week. The App Review notes in App Store Connect say that the Sandbox's week is small on purpose.
- **Why:** with the weekly usage the packs show only once about 140 questions are used: App Review could not find them, and in-app purchases it cannot find are a rejection under guideline 2.1.

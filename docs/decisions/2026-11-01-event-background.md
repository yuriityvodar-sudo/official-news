# Events from Wikipedia get the background of their story (#230)

- **Decision:** an event from Wikipedia's current events is stored with a Background: the lead of the Wikipedia article its topic lines link (the closest topic above it, the link whose words the event's sentence shares most; without topics its own link), read once per title a run from `/wiki/<title>?action=raw`. The event prompt writes 2 to 5 sentences, up to 700 characters: first the event as its sentence tells it, then one to three sentences from the Background (who or what it is, what led to it), never presenting the Background as news; headline and short summary tell the event only. Replaces 30 Oct's "1 to 3 short sentences, up to 400 characters" (#244). The text is Wikipedia's, CC BY-SA 4.0, like the event's own line.
- **Why:** Roman, 1 Nov: «почему новости из википедии такие короткие?». An event is one sentence on the portal, and the prompt allowed nothing else, so the live summaries were 88 to 286 characters, a retold sentence. The topics above each event link Wikipedia's article on its story ("Blockade of Oleshky" above the Oleshky evacuation). Tried on the summarizer's model with two live events: Oleshky 289 → 375 characters (the food crisis, the population down from 25,000 to 2,000), the French protests 142 → 438 (since September, who takes part, why).
- **Status:** Proposed (Roman's Claude, on Roman's word of 1 Nov)

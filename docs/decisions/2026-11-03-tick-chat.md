# A tick opens the same AI chat, about its event (#230, #146)

- **Decision:** A tick (a world story, `GET /ticks`) opens the AI chat we have (#146): the same subscription, daily limit, packs, consent, Sandbox key and screen. `POST /v1/chat` and `/v1/chat/status` take `tickId` in place of `articleId`; the agent, the same analyst with the same rules, gets the event's world story from the last build: the model's headline in English and in the reader's language, its countries, and up to 20 of its outlets' latest headlines with their outlets, countries and times, never their texts, and is told that these headlines are all it has of the event, which is newer than what it knows, so it never fills the gap. A question about a tick takes 1 unit of the limit. A tick the last build has none of answers 404.
- **Why:** Roman, 3 Nov, asked whether the chat for ticks should be its own: «чат такой же как у нас уже есть». The outlets' headlines are what we may use of other media (the fixed principle: a headline and a link, never their text), so they are the agent's whole source on the event; its own knowledge stops before it.
- **Status:** Proposed (Roman's Claude, on Roman's word of 3 Nov)

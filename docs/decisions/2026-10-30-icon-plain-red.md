# The N's red window is one even red

- **Decision:** the red window on the N, in the app icon (all 19 sizes) and the N in the feed's masthead (`app/assets/logo_n.png`, 1x–3x), is one even red, RGB (232, 4, 5), the clean red of the window's own edge. Its darker middle and diagonal shade are gone; the white rim highlights and the black border stay. The dark-mode N (`logo_n_dark.png`) is unchanged.
- **Why:** Yurii, 30 Oct: the shaded middle looked like a dark spot; he chose plain red over versions with a white "News" or "NEWS" in the window (shown on the simulator's home screen, where the word was too small to read).
- **Status:** Agreed (Yurii, 30 Oct).

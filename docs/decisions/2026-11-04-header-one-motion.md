# The masthead folds in one soft motion; the buttons go too; the edge comes in by its blur (#141)

- **Decision:** in the Garnet and Seal looks the masthead folds as one motion over the same 40 points of scroll, eased at both ends (smoothstep), so nothing starts or stops with a jolt and everything lands together. The title shrinks to 62% into the 44-point bar, as before. The date rides up with the page, slides under the title's foot and fades, gone at 90% of the fold. The buttons go too: each shrinks away around its middle with the fold, takes no tap once half gone and is not drawn once gone, so the folded bar holds the title alone; they come back as the feed returns to the top. Right after the fold, while the rows pass under the pills to the bar, iOS's soft edge grows from nothing to its 45%: Flutter tells iOS's blur how strong to be (`shown`, on the channel `official_news/edge_<id>`) and never fades it by an alpha. Replaces, in `2026-11-04-header-shrinks.md`, the date gone halfway, the buttons staying on the right in the bar, and the edge showing once the rows come under the bar.
- **Why:** Roman, 4 Nov: «не нравится наша шторка и сама шапка, как-то неорганично двигается, и есть несколько стадий, где видно резкое движение, а не плавное соотносительно скролла». The fold had straight stages that each started and stopped dead: the date over 20 points, the title over 40, nothing until 68, then the edge to 92. The edge came in under Flutter's `Opacity`, and iOS draws a visual effect view under an alpha below 1 wrong or not at all, so it appeared at once at the end; at 0 Flutter also took iOS's view off the screen. Of `handoff/header-smooth.html` he took «плавно как в NYT» and added «кнопки тоже уходят». The buttons shrink rather than fade for the same reason as the edge: their Liquid Glass is iOS's too.
- **Status:** Proposed (Roman's Claude, on Roman's word of 4 Nov)

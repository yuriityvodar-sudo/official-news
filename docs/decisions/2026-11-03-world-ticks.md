# The world's stories, for the feed's ticks (#230, the aggregator step 4)

- **Decision:** An event that the outlets of 2 countries or more carry is a world story: each country's first stories with the model's headline (`story_titles`) are grouped by their English headlines, the way one country's headlines are grouped (`topStories`), so no AI call of their own; the world's outlets (WW) count as a country. One row (`world_stories`), built at most every 5 minutes after a country's stories, in a call of its own, from the countries' titled stories kept apart (`country_stories.top`); `GET /ticks?lang=xx` gives the 12 weightiest (each country 1, halved every 6 hours since its last headline on it), with the model's headline in the reader's language, the countries' stories on it, and up to 20 of its outlets' latest headlines. A world story keeps its id from build to build while most of its stories stay in it, so the chat about a tick finds it. The app (later pull request) shows them as ticks.
- **Why:** Roman, 3 Nov, wants «тики»: our AI's short headline of an event the world is talking about, with a way to discuss it with the AI, and no text of anyone else's. After several mockups: «ячейка должна быть тиком в ленте, а не полноценной частью ленты как другие ячейки», then variant 1 of `handoff/tick-strip.html`, «1-кнопка справа» (a glass capsule between the cards, the time first, the AI's mark on the right; a tap opens the chat, with the outlets in its header). The model's English headlines make the countries' stories comparable without a new call: on 3 Nov at 10:00 UTC, 73 titled stories of 23 countries gave 6 world stories and none joined wrongly (the US strikes on Qeshm in 5 countries, a Tenerife footballer's death in 3); multilingual embeddings would find more (the US midterms' headlines told different sides of them) and stay the next step if these are too few. The build takes 2.2 ms of CPU cold on a laptop, of the 10 a call has, with each headline's words read by the country's build.
- **Status:** Proposed (Roman's Claude, on Roman's word of 3 Nov)

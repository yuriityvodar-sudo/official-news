# The splash: the N flies into the masthead (#141)

- **Decision:** the app starts with Roman's pick 1 of `handoff/intro-logo.html`, «Перелёт в шапку» (`SplashStyle.fly`), on the same plain page as the launch screen. The N springs in a little above the middle and its red window lights up. It then flies to the masthead's N, as wide as it, while the page fades into the feed, and gives way to the masthead's own N where it lands: the masthead's N is unseen until then (`SplashLogo`, `SplashLogoTarget`). The whole splash takes 1.5 s. The black N is on the white page and the red N on the black one, as the masthead has them. Both pictures are clear-background copies of the icon's N and of `docs/design/icon/outer-news-n-red.png`, 450 px wide (`app/assets/splash_n*.png`). With Reduce Motion the N only fades in and out in the middle. A touch or a notification ends it at once, as before. Without a masthead under it, the N stays in the middle and fades with the page. Replaces 1b of 20 Oct (Yurii's pick), the app's name rising; 1b and the other word styles stay for a build's `--dart-define=SPLASH`.
- **Why:** Roman, 4 Nov, of the six intros with the logo in `handoff/intro-logo.html`: «Intro logo 1 Перелёт в шапку». It is the shortest, and the logo does not vanish but takes its place on the screen, as iOS opens an app from its icon.
- **Status:** Proposed (Roman's Claude, on Roman's word of 4 Nov)

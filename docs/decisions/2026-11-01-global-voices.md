# Global Voices as a source of news stories, with their authors (#230)

- **Decision:** Global Voices (`ww-global-voices`, its English feed, CC BY 3.0) is a source of news stories: about 3 a day, every day, whole texts. Each item keeps its author (`articles.author`, from `dc:creator`; `author` in the API), no picture is taken from it (its photos are often other creators', on other terms), and its own announcements are left out. Stories are summarized with their own prompt (`storyPrompt`: what is happening, to whom and why it matters, views as their authors', no other outlet named) and typed `event`, not a decision. The source stays `hidden` (summarized, never shown) until the app and the site show "This story by {author} originally appeared on Global Voices" with the link and the licence at the top, as its republishing guidelines ask; that is the next pull request.
- **Why:** the plan of #230 (29 Oct) has Global Voices among the five sources; Roman, 1 Nov: «доработай» and «у нас должны быть всегда самые горячие обновления»: official sites publish little at weekends (16 articles in the 24 hours to Sunday morning), and Global Voices publishes every day. Its licence (CC BY) needs the author's name, which the fetcher did not keep before.
- **Status:** Proposed (Roman's Claude, on Roman's word of 1 Nov)

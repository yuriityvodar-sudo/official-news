# On the iPad the feed has columns, as Apple News, and the rest stands in a column in the middle (#137)

- **Decision:** a window 700 points wide or more is laid out wide (`app/lib/core/wide.dart`): the feed in two columns up to 1100 points and three wider, the cards of a row as tall as each other; the first of the Top stories across two columns and in larger type (26 instead of 18 points), as Apple News leads its Top Stories; the block's order stays the phone's. An article stands in a column of 680 points in the middle, its bar's buttons at the screen's edges, and grows out of its card with its own top there, not with the card where it stood among the columns. The AI chat has a column of 720 points; Settings, its pages, the Conversations and Contact & Support 680; the paywall and the top-up sheet 640. A narrower window, a phone included, is laid out as before. The columns are the Garnet and Seal looks' (the default); the other looks in Settings → Design keep one column. The layout follows the window's width, never the device: on iPadOS 26 a window can take any width.
- **Why:** Roman's iPad screenshots of 4 Nov showed the phone's layout stretched across 13 inches: cards and lines of 100 characters and more, the chat and Settings with half the screen empty. He picked A, «как Apple News», of `handoff/ipad-layout.html` on 5 Nov; B, «как Mail» (the feed on the left, the article or the chat on the right), was the other.
- **Status:** Proposed (Roman's Claude, on Roman's word of 5 Nov)

# A story's headline in the reader's language, and the stories in For you (#230, the aggregator step 3)

- **Decision:** A country's first 6 stories carried by 2 outlets or more get a headline in English, Ukrainian, Russian, Spanish and German, written once per story by one Workers AI call (the summarizer's model) from its outlets' own headlines: only what they say and agree on, no outlet named, plain words, at most 90 characters, names in Cyrillic in Ukrainian and Russian. The answer is checked (each in its language, no word left in Latin letters in Ukrainian and Russian, none over 120 characters) and kept per story (`story_titles`); an unusable answer is asked again, three times at most. `GET /stories` takes `lang` and says whether the title is the model's (`aiTitle`), and For you's first page gives the reader's country's first stories as `stories`, so the app (next pull request) shows them in «Главное для вас» as Roman picked: variant B of `handoff/stories-app.html`, two stories, then a decision.
- **Why:** Roman, 1 Nov: «B- как Apple News» and, on 19 headlines the model wrote on 1 Nov's stories (`handoff/aggregator/story-titles-trial-2026-11-01.json`), «Да, строй». The headlines read well with some slips («текстник», «об поражении»); the first trial left one English word in a Ukrainian headline, which the translator's check alone lets through, so this check refuses any. About 7 neurons a call, so about 2,300 a day for some 350 stories, within the free 10,000 with the summaries' 1,650 to 5,800. Groq's free translations are near their limits (#278), so this goes to Workers AI.

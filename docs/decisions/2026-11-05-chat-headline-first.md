# The AI chat: what it is about comes first (#146)

- **Decision:** the news's headline is the main thing on the chat's screen, and the chat fills the screen (Roman's pick C of `handoff/chat-designs.html`, like Apple Music).
  - **The cover:** the look's colour, deepening (Garnet or Seal, the same in both themes), across the top of the screen with rounded lower corners, under the status bar's white words. On it the headline, white and as big as in the article, with its source under it; the icon and "AI agent" stand small over them, beside the close button. As the page scrolls, the cover folds into a band in the same colour, with the same headline on up to three lines. This replaces the bar with "AI agent" over a small grey headline.
  - **The conversation:** the first question stays under the cover, so the headline is still there when the answer comes; each later question goes to the top, 16 points under the band. The reader's bubble is a tint of the colour, not filled with it, so it does not outdo the headline. Only the notice folds once a question is asked. A conversation with one question opens at the top of the page; a longer one at its last question, the cover already folded. The rest of 30 Oct's "The AI chat moves like ChatGPT's" (`docs/decisions.md`) stays.
  - **The field:** a capsule of iOS's glass floating low over the foot, with a thin edge and a soft shadow, because the glass alone hardly shows on the white page. The page fades out towards it instead of being cut off, and goes on under it, so the text shows through the glass. Its send button is grey until there is something to send. Over the field the usage line is a small grey glass capsule, no longer red. The suggested questions stand in a row over the field until the first is asked, not under the headline.
  - **The keyboard:** it closes when the page is dragged, on a tap outside the field, and when a question is sent with the button, as with the return key.
  - **The release:** it goes in the first release (Roman, 5 Nov: «С новым чатом»), so store build 63, which does not have it, is not submitted; the next `store` build from main is.
- **Why:** Roman, 5 Nov, on the chat as it was: «в первую очередь должно быть понятно о чем чат», the headline has the main value, the icon and the name come second, and the chat should be more beautiful and fill the screen.
- **Status:** Proposed (Roman's Claude, on Roman's word of 5 Nov)

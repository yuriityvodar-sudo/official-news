# Each country's headlines grouped into stories, and ranked for that country (#230, the aggregator step 2)

- **Decision:** A country's outlets' headlines of the last 24 hours are grouped by event into stories, and the stories ranked for that country: each outlet on a story counts 1, halved every 6 hours since its last headline on it, so what many of the country's outlets report now comes first. The grouping is the same for every country and language, with no word lists: words cut to their first 5 letters (so "Sněžka" and "Sněžkou" meet), scripts without spaces and Hangul compared by pairs of characters, a word weighing by how rare it is among the country's headlines and a name 1.5 times; a headline joins the story it is most like (a cosine of 0.34 or more), and a headline left alone looks again once the stories have grown. Each country's stories are one D1 row (`country_stories`), built at the end of the summarizer's and the translator's runs when its outlets have new headlines, each country in a call of its own; `GET /stories?country=XX` reads that one row. The app shows them in the next step (story cards, the story's page with each outlet's own headline and link).
- **Why:** Roman, 1 Nov: «для пользователя из разной страны свой источник и свое ранжирование / так как это делают агрегаторы». On the headlines of 1 Nov: Czechia's top stories were the death of Pavel Žák and the hotel fire in Pec pod Sněžkou, 4 outlets each; Germany's the FBI on North Korea's Binance hack (5), Ukraine's the Kremlin and Kallas on Putin at the G20; 287 stories carried by 2 outlets or more in 23 countries and groups. The thresholds come from those headlines: 0.30 joined unrelated ones (two shootings, "Schlagzeilen" and "Schneller Schlau"), 0.38 lost real ones; a name weight of 2 let two-word names ("New York", "Supreme Court") join unrelated stories, and 1.5 got 8 of 10 hand-marked pairs right. At most 7 ms of CPU for the biggest country (India, 439 headlines, laptop), within a call's 10 ms. No AI call: the story's headline in the reader's language is step 3.

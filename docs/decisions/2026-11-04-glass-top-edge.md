# The feed's rows go on under the status bar, under iOS's soft edge (#141)

- **Decision:** in the Garnet and Seal looks the feed no longer cuts its rows where the screen's safe area starts. As the feed scrolls, the rows go on under the pinned pills and under the status bar, and iOS's soft scroll edge blurs them there. It is a native blur (`EdgeView` in the Runner, the system's thinnest material, iOS 15 and later) from the screen's top to 24 points below the pills. It fades out over those 24 points, and fades in over the last 24 points before the rows reach the pills. On Android and in tests the page's colour fades the same way. The pull to refresh's words now stand in the middle of the screen, not at its left. This changes the end of the pinned header (`_NewsHeader`): until now the rows were cut in a straight line under the status bar, and only the page's tint showed above them.
- **Why:** Roman, 4 Nov: «у нас есть вверху полоса или там заканчивается наш контент / сделай как в iOS 26 вверху красивая стеклянная шторка, которая размывает контент что под ней / также перемести на центр текст подсказку release to refresh». The cut showed as a band under the status bar.
- **Status:** Proposed (Roman's Claude, on Roman's word of 4 Nov)

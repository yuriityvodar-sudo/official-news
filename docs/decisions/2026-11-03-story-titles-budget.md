# The stories' headlines within an hourly limit, so the summaries never wait (#230)

- **Decision:** The stories' AI headlines (`story_titles`, 2026-11-01-story-titles.md) take at most 24 Workers AI calls an hour of the clock, all countries together, counted in `ai_calls`: about 4,000 neurons a day, so the summaries, about 2,000 a day, always have room in the free 10,000. A story that comes again under a new id (its first headline left the build's window) keeps the headline it had: its titles are kept under each of its outlets' headlines too, and a story without titles takes those that at least half of its headlines hold, with no call. Past the hour's limit a story shows its outlets' own headline (`aiTitle: false`) until a later hour writes it. After Yurii's review of #290: each call takes its place in the hour first, in one statement (an upsert that adds 1 only below 24, `RETURNING`), so builds side by side never pass the limit, and a place taken counts whatever the answer, an error of Workers AI too (while it refuses, an hour's tries end at 24); and no story gets a headline while an item of the last day has waited 10 minutes for its summary, so the summaries come first on any day.
- **Why:** The day's free neurons ran out at about 08:30 UTC on 3 Nov: from then every summary and headline call failed with `4006: you have used up your daily free allocation of 10,000 neurons` (live tail, 10:04 UTC), so new decisions waited for their summaries (none is lost: an error of Workers AI costs no attempt). The headlines took about 1,000 calls a day (929 written from 2 Nov 14:00 to 3 Nov 09:30 UTC, about 1,030 calls), not the 350 the trial of 1 Nov counted, since a country's first stories change all day; Workers AI's own count (GraphQL) went from about 2,000 neurons a day before them to about 7,200 on 2 Nov. Of the 929, 158 told a story of the same country again under a new id (KR "Lee Hwa-young requests retrial" 3 times in one night). Cloudflare's pricing page says its limits reset at 00:00 UTC, but by its own count (GraphQL, at the model's 9,091 neurons a million input tokens and 27,273 a million output) 3 Nov had about 3,500 when the calls began to fail, and the 24 hours from 2 Nov 08:30 about 10,000 (6,645 on 2 Nov after 08:30, about 3,400 on 3 Nov): the last 24 hours count. So the limit is an hour's, which keeps any 24 hours under it: 24 headlines an hour and the summaries' busiest days (about 100 decisions of up to 40 neurons) make about 8,000.
- **Status:** Agreed (Yurii, 3 Nov, approving #290; the exact limit and the summaries first are his review's two notes)

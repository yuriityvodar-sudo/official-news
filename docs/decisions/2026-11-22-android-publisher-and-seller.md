# Android: RS Elite Pro publishes the news, Yurii sells the app on Google Play

**Decision (22 Nov 2026, #345).** On Google Play, the "News and magazine apps" declaration names **RS Elite Pro s.r.o.** as the entity behind the news (as on the App Store and the website), with https://outernews.app/en/support as its contact page. The app is distributed and sold there by **Yurii Tyvodar**, whose Play account it is; payments made on Google Play go to him. RS Elite Pro stays the data controller. The privacy policy says so in one line (`privacyGooglePlay`, all five languages, app and website), dated 23 Nov 2026.

**Why.** Google asks news apps who is behind the content, and asks that the privacy policy name the developer shown on the Play listing. Proposed by Yurii on 19 Nov; Roman agreed on 22 Nov («Да, отправь»).

**Status.** Agreed. The Play declaration is filled in once this line is live on the website.

# The website's funnel to the App Store, counted

- **Decision:** we count the way from the website to the app in Google Analytics and in Apple's own reports, as AlphaMingo and LyFi do (Yurii, 3 Nov).
  1. **Visitors:** Google Analytics' web stream (after the cookie wall's yes), plus Google Search's clicks, impressions, position and queries from Search Console, which Google counts without cookies. The admin page's service account `analytics-reader` reads Search Console (Restricted user, added 3 Nov).
  2. **Clicks on the App Store button:** the button links to our `/go/appstore/<channel>`, which sends the visitor to the App Store with Apple's campaign tokens (`pt`, our provider; `ct`, the channel `web-<page kind>-<lang>`). Google Analytics counts the click as `app_store_click` with its store and channel (after the yes). The redirect is never cached and robots.txt keeps crawlers out of it.
  3. **App Store page views and downloads from the website:** App Store Connect › App Analytics › Campaigns, per channel, and Web Referrer `outernews.app`. Apple shows a campaign once it has 5 or more downloads, and a day or two late. It never tells the app who installed from where, so this is counted per channel, never per person.
  4. **Using the app:** Google Analytics' app stream: daily active users, new users (`first_open`), sessions a day and per user, engagement, returning users, for the App Store build only (`build = store`).
  5. **A daily report** on the admin page, as LyFi's: yesterday against the day before, 7 days against the 7 before, 30 against the 30 before, in Kyiv time.
- **Why:** Yurii wants proven numbers for how many people we acquire from the website and from Google, and how many use the app each day. AlphaMingo's lessons: without `pt`, Apple credited nothing for 90 days; raw redirect hits are mostly crawlers. LyFi's: the window and the comparison, each number with its unit.
- **Before the release:** `APP_STORE_PROVIDER` (`server/src/site/stores.ts`) is `129371207` since 3 Nov, the developer account's `pt` from App Store Connect's campaign link generator (not a secret; the same for every app of the account). `APP_STORE_LIVE` becomes true at the release. Apple's numbers (3) come into the report through an App Store Connect key with access to Sales and Analytics reports, which Yurii creates and puts in the admin Worker himself.
- **Privacy:** no new data about a visitor. The click event is Google Analytics' outbound link, which the policy already names, and it is sent only after the yes. The redirect stores nothing.
- **Status:** Agreed (Yurii, 3 Nov). Built in steps: the button and redirect (this PR), the daily report, then Apple's numbers.

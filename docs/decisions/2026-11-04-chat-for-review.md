# The AI chat opens in the App Store build, and App Review's Sandbox purchases get answers (#146, #137)

- **Decision:** the AI chat is in every iOS build, the `store` build submitted to App Review among them; `ChatScreen.opened` is gone. App Review buys in the Sandbox, and the `store` build has no Sandbox key, so the server answers a Sandbox subscription or pack without a key too. All Sandbox questions together get at most 100 a day (`SANDBOX_DAILY_QUESTIONS` in `server/src/chat/index.ts`): past that, a Sandbox subscription without a key gets `429` ("No questions left today") until midnight UTC. The team's keys (`CHAT_SANDBOX_KEY` for Roman's local builds, `CHAT_SANDBOX_KEY_TESTFLIGHT` for `testflight` builds) no longer open the chat; they free those builds from the cap. Everything else stays: Sandbox questions take the same daily limit as paid ones, a Sandbox subscription gets one free pack a day, and a pack never goes into a subscription from the other store. This replaces 28 Oct's "Sandbox purchases get the chat only on Roman's own builds" and the part of 29 Oct's decision on TestFlight that kept the chat closed in `store` builds (`docs/decisions.md`).
- **Why:** Roman, 4 Nov: «открыть sandbox для appreview». Apple asks a production server to accept Sandbox purchases so App Review can test them. Without a key there is no telling App Review's Sandbox purchases from anyone else's, and they cost nothing, so a cap bounds what they cost. At about $0.002 a question that is about $0.20 a day, and at most about $1.60 should every question be as long as the limit allows. App Review asks a few questions, well under the cap.
- **Status:** Proposed (Roman's Claude, on Roman's word of 4 Nov)

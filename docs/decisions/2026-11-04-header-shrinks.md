# The masthead's title shrinks into a bar as the feed scrolls; the top edge lower and lighter (#141)

- **Decision:** in the Garnet and Seal looks the masthead keeps its layout: the N and "Outer News" on the left, the date under them, the buttons on the right. Only its motion changes. As the feed scrolls, the title shrinks with the scroll, without a jump, to 62% in a 44-point bar under the status bar. The date goes first: it is gone halfway. The buttons stay on the right and move up into the bar. The pills stay pinned under the bar. iOS's soft edge (`GlassEdge`) now covers only the status bar and the bar, 16 points past the bar's foot. It shows once the rows come under the bar, at 45% of the material's strength. The pills keep their own glass. Replaces, for these two looks, the title and the buttons scrolling away, and the reach of `2026-11-04-glass-top-edge.md` (to 24 points below the pills, the material in full).
- **Why:** Roman, 4 Nov, on the build of #308: «шторка слишком высокая и темная». Of `handoff/header-motion.html` he took B's motion for our own layout: «шапка пусть остается где была только сам motion changes / Название уменьшается вместе с прокруткой, без рывков. / Дата уходит первой. / Кнопки остаются справа».

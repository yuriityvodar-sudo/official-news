# The masthead scrolls away as Apple News's does; the pills stop under the status bar; a pull leaves it at the top and grows it (#141)

- **Decision:** in the Garnet and Seal looks the masthead no longer folds. The title, the date and the buttons are the top of the page. They go up with it exactly as the feed scrolls, pass under iOS's soft edge over the status bar, and are not drawn once they are above the screen. That edge is the only blur: it is as tall as the safe area, and its last 16 points fade. It comes in over the first 24 points of scroll, as the page goes under it. Flutter still sets its strength on its channel (`official_news/edge_<id>`). The pills ride up with the page until they reach the status bar, then stay there as glass while the rows pass under them, with no blur of their own. Pulled down, the masthead stays at the top: it is the feed's first sliver and pinned, and the pull to refresh opens its gap under it (`FeedLook.refreshUnderMasthead`). The grey words stand in that gap ("Pull to refresh", "Release to refresh", then how it went). The title and the date grow from their top left as far as the feed is pulled, at the same rate as before (9% at 100 points, at most 12.6%), and the pills move down by as much as they grow. This replaces `2026-11-04-header-one-motion.md` (the fold into a 44-point bar, the buttons shrinking away, the edge under the pills). From `2026-11-04-glass-top-edge.md` it replaces the blur under the pills and the gap above the masthead.
- **Why:** Roman, 4 Nov, on #315's fold on the simulator: «плохо … шторка должна быть максимально маленькая чтобы только закрыть safearea вверху / header вообще весь также должен уходить». After seven variants with effects (`handoff/header-*.html`) he asked for «просто обычное стандартный уход header в стиле apple news или что-то подобное как sticky header то что уже делают лидеры iOS 26». On the mockups he asked «оставь просто саму полосу как была в apple news и просто чтобы ряд пилюль в нее упирался, а когда тянем вниз то чтобы был еффект sticky and scale», and then «оставь только одну полосу как шторку блур высотой safearea, все остальное мне нравится» (`handoff/header-news-sticky.html`).
- **Status:** Proposed (Roman's Claude, on Roman's word of 4 Nov)

# News from free outlets fills the feed: Radio Svoboda, RFE/RL and Global Voices, each with its credit (#230)

- **Decision:** Three feeds of Radio Free Europe/Radio Liberty become sources of news stories, beside Global Voices, which is shown now: Radio Svoboda's Ukrainian Important news (`ua-radio-svoboda`, about 12 a day), its Russian Most important events of the day (`ww-radio-svoboda-ru`, about 5; its source country is the world, as RFE/RL's and Global Voices', since Russia is no source country, #42) and RFE/RL's English stories (`ww-rferl`, about 6 at a weekend). RFE/RL's Use Our Content allows reusing its text with a permanent link to the original before it and its copyright line; it forbids selling the content, ads and AI training, and licenses photos apart, so no picture is taken. Stories are summarized and translated like events (the story prompt), so each costs about 120 neurons of Workers AI with its translations: editors' picks rather than every item keep the day's AI within its free 10,000 neurons (1,650 to 5,800 used a day in the last week). The API gives every article its source's `credit` (`docs/source-credits.csv`: licence, its link, the exact wording, a `{year}` in it the article's) and a story's `author` (never a decision's: only a `stories` source keeps one, and RSS's "address (Name)" gives the name). The app and the site show a story with its author and outlet linking the original, the wording and the licence before its text, "Read on {source}" for its link and "Summary by AI of {source}'s text" for its label, where a Wikipedia event names Wikipedia.
- **Why:** Roman, 1 Nov, after the check of 25 outlets (most big ones allow their feeds only for personal, non-commercial use, or need a paid licence): «пользуем то что есть бесплатно без проблем или сложностей / главное чтобы лента была всегда заполнялась свежими новостями вместе с тем что у нас уже есть». Official sites publish little at night and at weekends (16 articles in the 24 hours to Sunday morning), and these outlets publish every day. Left out: UN News (its terms ask to advise the UN of each use), Meduza (only its war coverage is CC BY), Ukrinform (mobile apps need its written consent), Ukrainska Pravda (forbids any commercial use), and every outlet that allows only personal use.
- **Status:** Proposed (Roman's Claude, on Roman's word of 1 Nov)

# Usage statistics in the app only once the reader allows them (#137)

- **Decision:** the app sends usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase only once the reader allows it. Firebase still starts at launch for push notifications, but it collects and keeps nothing for analytics until then. On iOS this is set in Info.plist (`FIREBASE_ANALYTICS_COLLECTION_ENABLED`, `GOOGLE_ANALYTICS_DEFAULT_ALLOW_ANALYTICS_STORAGE` off), on Android in the manifest. The app asks once, after the splash on the first launch, in a plain iOS alert: «Помочь улучшить Outer News?» with «Не разрешать» and «Разрешить». The two answers weigh the same, as in iOS's own tracking question. The answer is kept on the phone (`analytics.consent`). Settings gets a «Статистика использования» switch that turns it on or off at once. On, Firebase's analytics storage consent is granted and the ad consents stay denied. A build without Firebase never asks. The privacy policy says so in all 5 languages, on the site too, and its date is now 4 Nov 2026. Replaces 16 Oct's (#60) analytics switched on at every launch.
- **Why:** L1 of the release audit (`handoff/audit-2026-11-04.html`, 4 Nov). In the EU, storing or reading an identifier on the phone for statistics needs consent first (the ePrivacy Directive, art. 5(3); in Czechia § 89(3) of Act 127/2005 Coll., opt-in since 2022), and the app ID Firebase keeps is one. The website already asks; the app did not. Roman, 4 Nov: «закрывай все блокеры».

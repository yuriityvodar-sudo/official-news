# Headlines the AI wrote carry ✦ in the feed (#137)

- **Decision:** on the feed's cards in Garnet and Seal, a small ✦ in the time's grey follows the time wherever the AI wrote the headline, with no words:
  - a decision whose summary is written; until then the card shows the source's own title, without the mark;
  - every tick;
  - a story with the model's headline (`aiTitle`), and not a story still showing its outlets' own headline.

  VoiceOver adds "Headline by AI" ("Заголовок от ИИ") to the card. Settings' footer says what ✦ means, in the 5 languages: «Краткие изложения и заголовки со значком ✦ пишет ИИ…». The article page keeps its "Summary by AI" label. The website already marks its AI text in the page's description, its link preview's title and its JSON-LD (`creditText`), and the API shows which text is the model's (`headline` beside `originalTitle`, `aiTitle`), so nothing changes there.
- **Why:** L2 of the release audit (`handoff/audit-2026-11-04.html`, 4 Nov). Article 50(4) of the EU's AI Act has applied since 2 Aug 2026 and was not postponed by the Digital Omnibus. It wants text an AI wrote to inform the public on matters of public interest shown as such, unless a person reviews it. Fines go up to €15 million or 3% of turnover. The ticks' and the stories' headlines are the model's, and their "Headline by AI" was no longer shown anywhere since #307. A mark without words keeps Roman's rule of no secondary texts (1 Nov). Roman, 4 Nov: «закрывай все блокеры».

# The reader's country's stories in the app (#230, the aggregator step 3)

- **Decision:** In For you, «Главное для вас» shows the reader's country's stories (For you's `stories`): two stories, then one of the block's decisions, and again; the stories left after the last decision come under it. In the Garnet and Seal looks a story's card is a decision's card (Roman's variant B of `handoff/stories-app.html`, after Apple News): the country and the outlets where a decision has its country and institution ("Чехия · ČT24, Seznam Zprávy и ещё 2", no seal), the story's headline, the time of its latest headline (in the look's colour within the hour, as a decision's), and in the picture's place a tile with the number of outlets in the look's gradient, «СМИ» under it and the first four outlets' initials on iOS's grey for a contact without a photo (the API gives outlets no colours). No topic tags. The other looks (Now, A, B, C) show no stories. A story opens its page, iOS's push with the swipe back: "ЧЕХИЯ · СЮЖЕТ", the headline in the article's serif, «Заголовок от ИИ» only when the model wrote it, then one card per outlet with its initials and name, its own headline, its time and «Читать на …», which opens the outlet's page in the in-app browser. Nothing more: no heading over the outlets, no footnote, no first or last report line. Its screen in analytics is `story`.
- **Why:** Roman, 1 Nov, on the mockup: «B- как Apple News», then «только не надо писать "4 сми об этом" / такие другорядные тексты нам не нужны», and «Да, строй». Garnet is the default look; the others are alternatives in Settings → Design that the mockup did not cover, so they get no story card of a design nobody picked.
- **Status:** Proposed (Roman's Claude, on Roman's word of 1 Nov)

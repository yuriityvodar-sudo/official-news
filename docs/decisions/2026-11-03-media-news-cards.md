- **Decision:** in For you's Top stories block the world's ticks and the reader's country's stories are one kind of card, the media's news: a decision's card, with where a decision has its seal and institution the place and the outlets ("World · 4 countries", "Czechia · Deník N, Echo24"), then the model's headline and the time, and on the time's line, at the right, a small "Discuss with AI" button (Roman's pick 1 of `handoff/ask-ai-button.html`). The card and its button both open the AI chat about it, the chat a decision and a tick have, behind its paywall; where the chat is not open yet (Android, a build before its release), the page of its outlets' headlines. A story's chat reads the story from its country's last build (`storyId` with `country` in `POST /v1/chat`, `docs/contract.md`).

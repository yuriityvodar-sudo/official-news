| 2026-10-15 | Translations (#46), after the MVP, consistency first: a separate translator step translates each news article's headline, short summary and summary into Ukrainian and Russian (`article_translations`, migration `0007`). `?lang=uk` or `?lang=ru` returns only articles fully translated into that language, never English mixed in, and `/articles/:id?lang=` is 404 until then. Each language has its own order, `lang_seq`, given in the statement that stores an article's first translation (MAX + 1), so a late translation lands on top of that language's feed; the API returns it as the article's `feedSeq` in that feed (and `translated_at` as `addedAt`), so the app (#51) reads every feed the same way, and `after=` and cursors use it. All or nothing: the three fields are checked together and stored in one statement (a CHECK constraint refuses a partial row). No missing information: every number of the English must be in the translation, each by a number of its own, compared by value in the language's format (a comma is the Ukrainian and Russian decimal mark, so English "1,500" copied unchanged is missing; a numeric date may name its month; 5pm may be 17:00, 17.00 or 17). The wrong-language check works field by field, paragraph by paragraph and sentence by sentence (Russian-only or Ukrainian-only letters and words, і/ї/є/ґ in every Ukrainian field of 100 letters or more, no sentence mostly in untranslated English), with names, acronyms, quoted titles and links allowed. A queue: every news article enters it for every language when its English is written or rewritten, and runs take the oldest English first, so a backlog comes out in each language in the English feed's order; a run with nothing to do reads about 13 D1 rows. An unusable answer is retried 10 minutes later, at most 3 times; then the article does not show in that language. A rate limit, quota or outage frees the claims without counting a try. A re-summarized article is translated again, and the old translation is served until the new one is stored, or until the new English has used its 3 tries: then the article no longer shows in that language. One AI call per language per article, a change to the fixed rule "one AI call per article", which still holds for the summary. Groq only for now, each language's model order in one constant (`TRANSLATORS` in `server/src/translator/index.ts`): Ukrainian `qwen/qwen3.8-27b` then `openai/gpt-oss-120b`; Russian `openai/gpt-oss-120b` then `qwen/qwen3.8-27b`; one language per request, `max_tokens` 700. No Workers AI for translations, so its free Neurons stay for the summaries. No cron trigger of its own: the translator joins the one-trigger step rotation of #50 | Yurii (15 Oct, #46): speed does not matter, consistency does; a post shows in a language only when it is fully and correctly translated. Roman agreed on #46 to a second AI call per article for translation (one per language), after the MVP, with the summarizer untouched. Yurii chose the models after the translation test (#43). On that test's 120 Ukrainian translations the number check rejects one, where GPT-OSS changed a date (19 November to 30 November), and the language check rejects none; still so after the review fixes of 15 Oct (#49), which closed gaps where a dropped number was found inside another one ("3,5" counted for a dropped "5"), a Russian field passed as Ukrainian, a paragraph or sentence stayed English, and an idle run read about 5 D1 rows per article in the feed. Hiding the old translation once a re-summarized article's new English has used its 3 tries follows "a post shows in a language only when it is fully and correctly translated" over "the old one stays until the new one is ready", since the new one will not come: proposed in #49 for Yurii to confirm. A newly added Cloudflare cron trigger never activated (#50); Groq counts the requested `max_tokens` against Qwen's free 1,000 output tokens a minute | Agreed |

| 2026-10-22 | Summaries as useful news, not a retelling (#133, Roman). The headline is a hook, strong but true: the server picks its kind for each article, at random but always the same for one article, `question` (a question to the reader and the answer) or `consequence`, or `contrast` (the two strongest numbers) when the text gives two amounts; the model writes `plain` instead for war, disasters, deaths and decisions that change nothing practical.

| 2026-10-15 | Translations (#46), after the MVP, consistency first: a separate translator step translates each news article's headline, short summary and summary into Ukrainian and Russian (`article_translations`, migration `0007`), and Spanish and German (row above). `?lang=uk` or `?lang=ru` returns only articles fully translated into that language, never English mixed in, and `/articles/:id?lang=` is 404 until then. Each language has its own order, `lang_seq`, given in the statement that stores an article's first translation (MAX + 1), so a late translation lands on top of that language's feed; the API returns it as the article's `feedSeq` in that feed (and `translated_at` as `addedAt`), so the app (#51) reads every feed the same way, and `after=` and cursors use it. All or nothing: the three fields are checked together and stored in one statement (a CHECK constraint refuses a partial row). No missing information: every number of the English must be in the translation, each by a number of its own, compared by value in the language's format (a comma is the Ukrainian and Russian decimal mark, so English "1,500" copied unchanged is missing; a numeric date may name its month; 5pm may be 17:00, 17.00 or 17; 16.30 hours may be 16:30; a word for millions or more is read with its number, so €2.86 billion may be 2.860 millones or 2 860 млн). The wrong-language check works field by field, paragraph by paragraph and sentence by sentence (Russian-only or Ukrainian-only letters and words, і/ї/є/ґ in every Ukrainian field of 100 letters or more, no sentence mostly in untranslated English), with names, acronyms, quoted titles and links allowed. An explicit queue (migration `0008`, `docs/queue.md`): a state per article and language, `queued` → `translating` → `published`, `retrying` until `next_attempt_at` with a growing wait, `failed` after 3 unusable answers with the reason, `updating` while a re-summarized article is redone (the old text stays visible until the new one is published), `skipped` when it left the feed; every change of state logged with its time by a trigger (`translation_events`, 30 days). Nothing lost: every news article enters it for every enabled language, also one paused or without its key; a claim older than 10 minutes goes back to `queued`; a rate limit, quota or outage makes it `retrying` (2, 4, 8 … 60 minutes) without counting a try. A switch per language (`translation_langs`: enabled, paused). `GET /queue` shows per language the count per state, the last 24 hours, the oldest waiting and the last published, about 20 D1 rows (counts kept by triggers in `translation_counts`); `server/scripts/queue.mjs` lists, shows an article's history, requeues one item or a whole language, pauses, resumes, enables (with the 7-day backlog) and disables. Runs take one article per language, the oldest English first, so a backlog comes out in each language in the English feed's order and one language's backlog or outage never holds back another (review of #49: taking the 4 oldest in all let a Z.ai outage or a newly enabled language stop the others); an article that left the feed takes no place in a run; a run with nothing to do reads about 55 D1 rows, and one article in four languages writes about 130. An unusable answer is retried 10, then 20 minutes later, at most 3 times; then the article does not show in that language. A re-summarized article is translated again, and the old translation is served until the new one is stored, or until the new English has used its 3 tries: then the article no longer shows in that language. One AI call per language per article, a change to the fixed rule "one AI call per article", which still holds for the summary. Groq for Ukrainian and Russian, each language's model order in one constant (`TRANSLATORS` in `server/src/translator/index.ts`): Ukrainian `qwen/qwen3.8-27b` then `openai/gpt-oss-120b`; Russian `openai/gpt-oss-120b` then `qwen/qwen3.8-27b`; one language per request, `max_tokens` 700. No Workers AI for translations, so its free Neurons stay for the summaries. Its own cron trigger, every even minute (`0-58/2 * * * *`), up to 4 translations a run (one article in each language) | Yurii (15 Oct, #46): speed does not matter, consistency does; a post shows in a language only when it is fully and correctly translated; and the queue is the centre of the design, explicit and manageable. Roman agreed on #46 to a second AI call per article for translation (one per language), after the MVP, with the summarizer untouched. Yurii chose the models after the translation test (#43). On that test's 120 Ukrainian translations the number check rejects one, where GPT-OSS changed a date (19 November to 30 November), and the language check rejects none; still so after the review fixes of 15 Oct (#49), which closed gaps where a dropped number was found inside another one ("3,5" counted for a dropped "5"), a Russian field passed as Ukrainian, a paragraph or sentence stayed English, and an idle run read about 5 D1 rows per article in the feed. A translation requeued by hand for the same English (after a prompt change) stays shown when its redo fails, since its text still matches the English, and a run stores its answer only while it holds the claim, so a requeue made meanwhile is not undone (review of #49). Hiding the old translation once a re-summarized article's new English has used its 3 tries follows "a post shows in a language only when it is fully and correctly translated" over "the old one stays until the new one is ready", since the new one will not come: proposed in #49 for Yurii to confirm. The summarizer's new cron trigger started firing about 55 minutes after its deploy (#50), so a trigger of its own works, given an hour; Groq counts the requested `max_tokens` against Qwen's free 1,000 output tokens a minute | Agreed |
